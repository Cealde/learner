What is a PC

How a Computer "Thinks"

A Computer is a very complex Calculator, it does billions of calculations every second but without instructions to do these calculations a computer is simply just paper weight.

1. The Parts

Tiny Light Switches

Visual: A toggle set to ON (1) and OFF (0).

Inside every chip are billions of microscopic switches. They can only be ON (1) or OFF (0). Everything on your screen is built from these two states.

2. The Rules

Strict Recipes

Visual: An explicit list of unbending commands.

Computers have zero common sense. They cannot guess. You must spell out every micro-step: open door, step forward, close door.

3. The Secret

Extreme Speed

Visual: Blur and speed lines showing relentless execution.

Why do they feel smart? Because they can read those steps and flip switches billions of times per second.

Takeaway: A computer is just an obedient machine following simple rules at lightning speed.
